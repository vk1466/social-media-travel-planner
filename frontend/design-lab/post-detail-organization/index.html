Real Post Cross-Linking Taxonomy

Six bi-directional connections inside the post

Organizing raw social content into an actionable, interconnected travel dossier.

📍

Place ↔ Map Pin Sync

Hovering or clicking any place card activates its corresponding numbered pin on the map and pans the viewport.

Spatial Feedback
💡

Tips ↔ Specific Places

Tips are not dumped in a generic list; each tip is explicitly attached to the place it applies to (e.g. reservation notice on Saiho-ji).

Direct Attribution
🗺️

Post Sequence ↔ Route Line

If the creator presents a multi-stop route, it renders in chronological order (Stop 1 → 2 → 3) with a route path connecting the pins.

Narrative Order
💬

Caption ↔ Place Highlights

Places mentioned in the creator's caption are interactive pills. Clicking a mention highlights its card and map pin.

Text Entity Links
↗️

Place ↔ Google Maps Navigation

Each place has a direct Google Maps action with exact coordinates for instant turn-by-turn navigation.

Actionable Link
📸

Cover ↔ Instagram Provenance

A crisp post cover image with creator avatar, post date, and a prominent "Watch Reel on Instagram ↗" external action.

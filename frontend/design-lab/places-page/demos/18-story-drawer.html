On keeping an atlas.

Every reel you save leaves a pin behind. This page collects them into one running document — filed by region, marked when you've been, kept when you haven't.

The atlas is intentionally flat. Continents, cities, and single cafés share the same visual weight. What matters is that they exist in your library, not how far away they are.

Filters, search, and grouping stay to the right. Nothing here changes them.

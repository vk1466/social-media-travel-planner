Geological Wonder
Suobuya Stone Forest (梭布垭石林景区) Enshi Prefecture, Hubei · 30.5771°N, 109.5729°E
Intuitive Summary A surreal 460-million-year-old Ordovician karst stone forest spanning 21 square kilometers in Enshi, Hubei. Once an ancient seabed, geological forces sculpted it into a maze of towering mossy limestone monoliths, underground chambers, and lush slot canyons.
Opening Hours
08:30 – 17:00 (Peak season: Apr – Oct)
Admission Fee
~¥78 – ¥120 (Includes internal shuttle bus)
Key Scenic Areas
Lotus Valley (莲花寨), Grinding Stone Ravine (磨子沟)
Transit from Enshi City
~54 km north (approx. 90-min drive or bus)
Creator FactWalk directly through ancient seabed strata where sedimentary stone layers date back 460 million years.
Footwear AdvisoryExpect hundreds of damp, mossy stone steps; sturdy non-slip walking shoes or trail runners are strictly essential.
Best Photography LightVisit between 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM when the midday sun penetrates the deep narrow ravines to create dramatic vertical light beams.
